03 / Play

Find the stillness
inside the motion.

A randomly rotating cube becomes a spatial puzzle. Your goal is to counter its motion until it settles.

Play on Android

Observe, adjust, repeat.

  1. Start a challenge.The cube begins with a random configuration of rotation rates.
  2. Choose your controls.Use the axis selection button to choose which motion your swipes affect.
  3. Counter the motion.Use swipes to adjust rates. Watch what slows down and what still moves.
  4. Settle the cube.Bring the remaining motion within the level’s stopping tolerance.

Grow into the dimensions.

Complete 3D to unlock 4D, then 5D, then 6D. Higher difficulty starts with faster motion and asks for a more precise stop.

Leaderboards let you compare best times. The challenge is a practical way to notice how several rotations can contribute to one visible movement.

Tracing is available in the exploratory Sandbox and Muse modes. Play keeps its focus on bringing the motion to rest.

Learn the motion before racing the timer.

In Sandbox, add and remove one rotation at a time. Notice how a small adjustment affects the projection. Then return to Play and use that observation to guide your next move.
